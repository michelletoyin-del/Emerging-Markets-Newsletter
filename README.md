# The Irony of Nigeria's Agricultural Sector

## Overview

This project examines an apparent contradiction in Nigeria's economy: despite agriculture's significant contribution to output and employment, the sector attracts a very small share of investment capital.

Using data from the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), the analysis explores:

- Agricultural capital importation trends
- Agriculture's share of total capital inflows
- The relationship between investment incentives and capital allocation
- The potential impact of monetary conditions on investment into productive sectors

## Research Question

Why does Nigeria's agricultural sector attract such a small share of investment capital despite its economic importance?

## Key Findings

- Agriculture receives a negligible share of total capital importation.
- Capital inflows into agriculture are highly volatile.
- Government securities have become increasingly attractive relative to riskier agricultural investments.
- Capital allocation may be influenced by broader incentive structures, not just sector-specific risks.
